10Methodology

How I approach a security assessment

A repeatable, structured workflow — always carried out with explicit authorization.

All testing is performed only against systems I own or have explicit, written permission to assess. Scope and rules of engagement are agreed before any activity begins.

  1. 01

    Scope & Reconnaissance

    Confirm authorization, rules of engagement, and in-scope targets.

  2. 02

    Asset Discovery

    Map domains, subdomains, hosts, and exposed services.

  3. 03

    Enumeration

    Fingerprint technologies, endpoints, and functionality.

  4. 04

    Vulnerability Identification

    Spot likely weaknesses across the attack surface.

  5. 05

    Manual Testing

    Validate findings by hand to reduce false positives.

  6. 06

    Exploitation Validation

    Safely prove impact within the agreed scope.

  7. 07

    Impact Analysis

    Assess business risk and realistic exploitation.

  8. 08

    Documentation & Reporting

    Record clear, reproducible findings.

  9. 09

    Remediation Recommendations

    Provide actionable, prioritized fixes.

  10. 10

    Retesting

    Verify that remediations resolve the issue.
